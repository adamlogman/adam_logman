About

Background

I'm a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer with a dual background in Computer Science and Mechanical Engineering — which probably explains why I care as much about whether a model actually ships as I do about the math behind it. Recent work spans deploying continent-scale air quality monitoring pipelines — blending satellite data with sparse sensor networks to predict PM2.5 levels across Africa — to engineering and evaluating RAG-based AI agents for enterprise use. I'm ranked in the top 1% of 200,000+ data scientists on Zindi, and off the clock I lead Google Developer Groups on Campus at the University of Khartoum, teaching machine learning, computer vision, and NLP to the next generation of developers.
